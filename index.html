SLICE BLAST

A free one-thumb stacking game for iPhone. Tap to drop each block — land it dead centre to keep your width and build a combo, then watch the top of the tower blast away.

How it plays

Perfect drops in a row trigger blasts that grow 3, 5, 7 blocks and beyond. Special Neon, Electric, Glass and Steel blocks shake things up, and coins earned in play buy upgrades and themes in the Workshop.
